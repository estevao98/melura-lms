# Melura — Brand Concept

Melura is the selected name for the educational platform built on Frappe Learning / Frappe LMS.

The name comes from a conceptual blend of two references from the world of intelligent and vocal birds:

- Melopsittacus undulatus, the scientific name of the budgerigar, associated with communication, repetition, sociability and learning by imitation.
- Menura, the genus of lyrebirds, known for their exceptional ability to imitate complex sounds from their environment.

Melura represents a learning platform that listens, organizes, repeats, adapts, transmits and transforms knowledge.

The core positioning:

Melura is an open platform to create, organize and scale learning experiences.

Brand personality:
- clear
- intelligent
- organized
- trustworthy
- human
- lightweight
- modular

The brand should avoid generic education clichés such as graduation caps, owls, light bulbs, generic clouds and childish mascots.

Visual identity status:
The final logo, color palette, typography and design system are not decided yet. Do not hard-code final brand colors or final typography in this feature.

Possible product architecture:
- Melura Studio
- Melura Live
- Melura Console
- Melura Flow
- Melura Pages
- Melura Assess
- Melura Connect

Trademark note:
Melura is the working brand name. Before commercial use, it must go through a manual trademark search in INPI, especially classes 41, 42 and 9, including phonetic and spelling variations such as Melura, Melora, Melury, Melura Software, Melura Tecnologia, Melura Educação and Melura Learning.
